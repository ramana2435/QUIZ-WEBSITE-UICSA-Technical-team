ABOUT UICSA

The University Institute of Computer Science and Applications (UICSA) is a premier academic branch at Guru Nanak University, dedicated to fostering excellence in computer science education and technology innovation.

UICSA provides a vibrant learning environment where students develop strong technical foundations, practical skills, and industry-ready expertise. Our branch actively encourages student participation in technical events, competitions, and collaborative learning initiatives.

The UICSA Technical Team organizes various academic and co-curricular events throughout the year, creating opportunities for students to showcase their talents, enhance their knowledge, and build strong peer networks within the computer science community.

ABOUT GURU NANAK UNIVERSITY

Guru Nanak University is a distinguished institution located in Hyderabad, committed to providing quality education across diverse academic disciplines. The university emphasizes holistic development, combining rigorous academics with practical learning experiences.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a student-centric approach, Guru Nanak University creates an environment conducive to academic excellence, research, innovation, and character building.

The university encourages students to participate actively in technical competitions, cultural events, and community service, fostering well-rounded individuals prepared to excel in their chosen fields and contribute meaningfully to society.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

The quiz is open to all registered students of the UICSA Branch at Guru Nanak University, Hyderabad. Make sure you complete the registration process through the official form link provided on this website.

Click the "REGISTER FOR QUIZ" button on this website. You will be redirected to an official Google Form where you need to fill in your details. Complete the form before the registration deadline.

The venue and mode (online/offline) are mentioned in the Event Details section above. Please check the details carefully and arrive on time if it's an offline event. For online mode, ensure you have a stable internet connection.

At the scheduled quiz time, click the "START QUIZ" button on this website. You will be redirected to the official external test portal where the quiz will be conducted. Make sure to log in with your registered credentials.

For offline quizzes, bring your valid college ID card, stationery if required, and any other items mentioned in the instructions. For online quizzes, ensure your device is charged and you have a backup internet connection if possible.

Please read the "Rules & Guidelines" section carefully. Key rules include: no communication with other participants, no unauthorized devices, complete the quiz within time limits, follow coordinator instructions, and maintain academic integrity throughout.
